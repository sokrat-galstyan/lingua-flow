# Workflow 04 — Review Performance and Prepare Next Cycle

**Trigger:** "Review progress", "I finished this cycle", "What should I focus on next?".

1. Read `state/`, the active cycle `overview.md`, completed lesson notes, and any learner-provided writing/exercise results. Respect `docs/PROJECT_CONTRACT.md` evidence rules.
2. Verify what was **reported** completed and what remains unknown. If learner claims whole cycle complete but specific records are absent, record the explicit confirmation, clearly labeled self-report; do not invent individual test scores.
3. Analyze separately: conceptual understanding; controlled form accuracy; independent writing accuracy; speaking automaticity; receptive comprehension; natural expression; pronunciation where evidence exists; workload sustainability.
4. Identify 3–6 highest-impact review priorities using recurring patterns and context. Do not automatically repeat entire earlier chapters.
5. Link review priorities to `U-NNN` curriculum IDs and propose ways they can recur in next cycle. Identify new units whose prerequisites seem satisfied, plus uncertainties requiring low-stakes checks.
6. If all sessions required by the batch plan are explicitly confirmed, mark current cycle `completed` and update ledger. Otherwise keep `in-progress` or `paused`; report missing work. Never force an exam before continuing.
7. Add a concise `Next-cycle handoff` section to `state/learning-record.md` (or update the most recent one without deleting history). Include completed items, evidence, unresolved issues, skipped content, source problems and recommended unit IDs.
8. State whether `workflows/02-generate-next-cycle.md` is ready to run; do not generate new lessons unless requested.
